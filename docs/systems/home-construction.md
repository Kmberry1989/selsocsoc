# Home Construction & Furnishing

**Status:** SHIPPED

## What it is

Each player owns a home in Cyclical City that they can build out and furnish: place furniture, and customize with home materials (flooring, carpet, wallpaper, fabric, metal finishes registered in the texture manifest).

## How it works for the player

- Players arrange their home and place furniture pieces from the catalog.
- Furniture is 3D: big furniture pieces are modeled (per the art direction, large furniture stays 3D while small decor is 2D sprites).
- Home materials (flooring, carpet, wallpaper, fabric, metal) are registered in the texture manifest and available to runtime consumers. Note: the bundled home UI currently has hard-coded material selections — supplied materials are registered but not yet forced into saved customization choices (see GAMEPLAY-AUDIT.md "Remaining risks").
- The home layout persists per player across sessions.

## Key code files

- `assets/furniture/` — the furniture catalog: 3D `.glb` pieces (sofa, bed, tables, chairs, dresser, bookcase, floor lamp, rugs, …) plus `manifest.json` (regenerated by `assets/furniture/generate-manifest.mjs`).
- `assets/textures/` — terrain/material texture slots; the expanded texture manifest covers flooring, carpet, wallpaper, fabric, and metal entries.
- `assets/artwork-manifest.json` — supplied artwork registry.
- `assets/world-layout.js`, `assets/world/` — world/town layout data.

## Where state lives

- Firestore `houses/{uid}` — the player's saved house record (layout, furnishings). Returning players are restored from it.
- See `assets/MULTIPLAYER-SETUP.md` and `assets/firebase-firestore.rules` for the exact rule coverage.

## NPCs involved

None directly — the home is the player's own space.

## Known limitations

- Home save/reload controls were source/static-reviewed but not destructively exercised end-to-end (GAMEPLAY-AUDIT.md). Treat the persistence path as needing a live two-session check before calling it fully proven.
